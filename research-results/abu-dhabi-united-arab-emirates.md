## Food Prices in Abu Dhabi

### Grocery Costs and Market Comparisons
The average monthly grocery expenditure ranges from **$41–$297 (AED 150–1,090)** for individuals and **$109–$817 (AED 400–3,000)** for families[1][5]. Key staple prices include:
- Rice: **$1.78/kg (AED 6.55)**[4]
- Eggs: **$3.41/dozen (AED 12.50)**[5]
- Chicken breasts: **$10.90/kg (AED 40)**[5]
- Tomatoes: **$1.42/kg (AED 5.21)**[4]

Supermarkets like **Carrefour** and **Lulu Hypermarket** offer competitive pricing, while premium stores such as **Waitrose** and **Spinneys** charge 15–20% more for imported goods[5]. Local markets provide cost advantages for seasonal produce:
- **Mina Fish Market**: Fresh hammour (local fish) at **$8.17/kg (AED 30)** vs. **$13.62/kg (AED 50)** in supermarkets[4].
- **Vegetable Souks**: Tomatoes at **$0.95/kg (AED 3.5)** vs. **$1.42/kg (AED 5.21)** retail[4].
