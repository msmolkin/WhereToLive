# Comprehensive Analysis of Living Costs and Lifestyle in Alexandria, Egypt  

Alexandria, Egypt's second-largest city, offers a unique blend of Mediterranean charm, historical significance, and affordability. This report provides a detailed examination of living costs and lifestyle factors, including food prices, transportation, housing, and political climate, tailored for expatriates and academic audiences. ## 1. Food Prices  

### Grocery Costs  
The average monthly grocery cost for a single person in Alexandria ranges from **$152** for basic necessities to **$383** for a family of four[8]. Staple items exhibit significant price variations between supermarkets and local markets:  

- **Bread**: A 500g loaf costs **$1.05** (33 EGP) in supermarkets vs. **$0.67** (21 EGP) in local markets[6][10].  
- **Chicken**: 1 kg of fillets averages **$7.18** (226 EGP) in supermarkets compared to **$6.50** (205 EGP) in markets[6][10].  
- **Seafood**: Fresh fish like tilapia costs **$2.59–$5.71/kg** (81.26–179.18 EGP) in markets, while supermarkets charge premiums of 20–30%[19].  

Fava beans, a dietary staple, retail for **$2.17–$3.38/kg** (67.90–105.62 EGP)[12]. Produce such as tomatoes (**$0.34/kg**) and oranges (**$0.35/kg**) are notably cheaper in local souks like Souk El-Attarine[8].  

### Dining Out  
- **Traditional Egyptian restaurants**: A meal of koshari or ful medames costs **$1.90–$3.80** (60–120 EGP)[6].  
- **Mid-range seafood restaurants**: A three-course meal at establishments like the Fish Market averages **$38–$76** (1,200–2,400 EGP) per person[18].  
- **Street food**: Sandwiches like taameya (falafel) cost **$0.32–$0.63** (10–20 EGP)[17].  

Supermarkets like Carrefour and Metro Market offer convenience but are **15–25% more expensive** than local markets for staples like rice and dairy[6][10].

## 2. Commuting Costs & Transportation Methods  

### Vehicle Ownership  
- **New cars**: A Toyota Corolla sedan costs **$52,952–$55,555** (1.64–1.75 million EGP), while luxury models like the BMW X5 exceed **$225,000** (7.1 million EGP)[13].  
- **Used cars**: A 2016 Skoda Octavia sells for **$777** (24,500 EGP), reflecting steep depreciation[14].  
- **Monthly ownership**: Fuel (**$0.29/L** or 9.72 EGP)[15], insurance (**$30–$50/month**), and maintenance (**$20–$100/month**) make car ownership costly for average earners[6][16].  

### Public Transportation  
- **Trams and microbuses**: Single tickets cost **$0.32** (10 EGP), with monthly passes at **$22.22** (700 EGP)[6].  
- **Taxis**: A 8 km ride averages **$3.91** (123 EGP), while ride-hailing services (e.g., Uber) charge **$0.95–$1.58/km** (30–50 EGP)[6][8].  

### Transportation Habits  
Alexandria’s **historic tram network**, operational since 1860, remains a primary mode for 40% of residents due to its affordability and coverage along the corniche[6]. Only **25% of households own cars**, as narrow streets and traffic congestion favor microbuses and walking[7][9]. The public transit network is comprehensive but often overcrowded, with informal routes supplementing official lines[6].
