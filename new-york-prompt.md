## Comprehensive Living Cost and Lifestyle Research for New York City

Please research and provide detailed information about living costs and lifestyle in New York City, USA, covering the following areas:

### 1. Food Prices
- Average monthly grocery cost for individuals and families
- Prices for common grocery items (bread, milk, eggs, rice, chicken, vegetables, fruit, etc.)
- Average cost of eating out (mid-range restaurant, fast food, coffee, soft drinks, bottled water)

### 2. Commuting Costs & Typical Transportation Methods
- Average price of a new and used car
- Monthly cost of car ownership (insurance, maintenance, parking, taxes)
- Average price of gasoline (per gallon)
- Cost of a single subway/bus ticket and monthly MTA pass
- Average cost of taxi and rideshare services (e.g., JFK airport to Manhattan)
- **Transportation Habits**: Describe how residents typically get around New York City:
  - What proportion of residents walk, use public transport, or drive cars?
  - Is car ownership common or necessary in different boroughs?
  - How effective is the subway system and other public transportation options?

### 3. Housing Costs & Rental Process
- Average price to buy a house/apartment (per square foot) in Manhattan and outer boroughs
- Average monthly rent for one- and three-bedroom apartments in different neighborhoods
- Average mortgage interest rates and typical monthly mortgage payments
- Required closing costs, taxes, and fees for purchasing property
- Typical upfront costs for renting (security deposit, first/last month's rent, broker fees, etc.)
- **Local Rental Process**: Describe how the rental system works in New York City:
  - Role of brokers and typical broker fees
  - Rent stabilization and rent control considerations
  - Application requirements and common practices
- Average monthly utilities cost (electricity, water, gas, internet)

### 4. Political Climate & Safety for Foreigners
- Current political climate in New York City and how it affects residents
- Safety considerations for foreigners, particularly in different neighborhoods
- Any visa or immigration considerations for foreigners looking to live in NYC
- Assessment of whether it's feasible for international citizens to live there safely and legally
- Any special considerations or precautions for foreigners

Please present all findings in a structured format with tables for easy comparison where appropriate. Include citations for all sources used in your research.
